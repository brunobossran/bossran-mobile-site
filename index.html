FAQ

Gotquestions?

When can I play?

We're building and testing now. Test dates will be posted first on our Facebook page. Join the test list and we'll email you when testing opens.

How will I get the test?

Android: we'll add your Google account to our Google Play test and email you a link. You install the game from the Play Store like any other app.

iPhone and iPad: we'll send an invite to your Apple ID email. Install Apple's free TestFlight app, then open the invite to install the game.

Which phones will be supported?

We're testing on a wide range of Android phones and iPhones. Add your phone model when you join the list so we can include it.

How do I contact you?

Email support@bossran.net or message our Facebook page.
