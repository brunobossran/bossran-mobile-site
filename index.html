          Gotquestions?

          Do I need an email invite?

          You can join yourself. On Android, download the APK or join through Google Play. On iPhone or iPad, open the TestFlight invitation on your device.

          Google Play opens a blank panel or says I’m ineligible. What do I do?

          Close the panel and tap your Play Store profile picture to check the full email address. Select the account you used for the tester group or invitation, then reopen step 2 and choose the same account. Or install the direct APK without tester signup.

          When can I play?

          Check Discord for play-session times.

          How do I contact you?

          Email support@bossran.net or message our Facebook page.
