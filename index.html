          Player help

          Create a game account

          For new players who want to make their first BossRan login.

          1. Choose Sign in, then select your sign-in method on the account page.
          2. Choose your game username and a password, then enter the password again.
          3. Tap Save game login and wait for the success message.
          4. Return to BossRan, choose Log in with username, and use the username and password you just saved.

          If that Google account already owns a BossRan account, you keep the same account.

          Sign in
          Recover a Google-linked account

          For returning players who used Google in the game and need to set or change their game password.

          1. Open the Recover game login link below.
          2. Sign in with the original Google account you used in BossRan.
          3. Keep your existing game username, or choose one if you never had one. For your first password, fill in the setup form and tap Save game login. Already have a password? Choose Change game password, enter your new password twice, and tap Save new password.
          4. Return to BossRan and choose Log in with username. Your original account, characters, items and balances stay with you.

          You do not need your old password after verifying your original Google account. If verification has expired, sign in with Google again. Recovery will not create a replacement account if you choose the wrong Google account.

          Recover game login
          Game username and password requirements

          New username: 4–12 characters. Start with a letter; use letters, numbers or an underscore.

          Username over 12 characters? Use the recovery link above and verify your original Google account. You can choose a shorter username once and set a new password. Tap Save corrected game login, then use your new login in the game. Your account, characters, items and balances stay the same.

          Password: 8–12 lowercase letters and numbers only. No capitals, spaces or special characters. Use a game password, not your Google password.

          After saving: saved game sign-ins on your phones expire. Use your game username and new password the next time you log in.

          Cannot access your original Google account? Contact support.

          Do I need an email invite?

          You can join yourself. On Android, download the APK or join through Google Play. On iPhone or iPad, open the TestFlight invitation on your device.

          Google Play opens a blank panel or says I’m ineligible. What do I do?

          Close the panel and tap your Play Store profile picture to check the full email address. Select the account you used for the tester group or invitation, then reopen step 2 and choose the same account. Or install the direct APK without tester signup.

          When can I play?

          The open beta is live and all items are free during the beta. Download BossRan Mobile. We’re targeting late November to early December 2026 for the official launch.

          How do I contact you?

          Email support@bossran.net or message our Facebook page.
